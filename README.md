# In the Trees

A kid gets carried off by birds and dropped in a nest at the top of a giant redwood. Get back down.

## [▶ Play In the Trees](https://in-the-trees.vercel.app/)

Controls: WASD move, mouse aims, left click shoots (hold to charge), right click throws bread, E eats bread, Space jumps, Shift dodges, 1/2/3 picks ammo.
